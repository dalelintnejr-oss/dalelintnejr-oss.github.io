Meet DJ & Mimi

✦ ✦ ✦

Your Storytellers

Amy & Dale Lintner

We're Amy and Dale — Mimi and DJ — a husband and wife team from Meadowbrook, PA, creating stories for the little ones we love. Our books are written for the children in our lives: Chloe, Josef, and all the kids who deserve to hear just how incredible they truly are.

Every story we write comes from the heart — because we believe the right words, whispered at just the right moment, can help a child discover the superpowers already inside them.
